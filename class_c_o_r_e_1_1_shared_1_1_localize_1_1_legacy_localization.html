Detailed Description

The GameToolkit-Localization era of CORE (LocalizedText assets, the LocalizeEnums enum). Everything that keeps it working is marked [Obsolete(LegacyLocalization.Message)] and is deleted in RemovedIn, together with ThirdParty/Localization; every piece of it references this class, so finding its usages finds all of it.

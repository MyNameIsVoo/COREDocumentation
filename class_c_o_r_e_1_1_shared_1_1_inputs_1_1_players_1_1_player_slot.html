Detailed Description

A player by index (since v1.3): a stable object whose subscriptions survive leaving and joining. The primary (index 0) is the host itself — its events are the host's, its actions the host's asset, keyboard and mouse are always its own. A player 1..N is joined while IsJoined: then it has a runtime copy of the assets restricted to its gamepad, a navigation context, haptics and, when the settings say so, a virtual cursor.

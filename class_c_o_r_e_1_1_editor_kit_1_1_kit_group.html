Detailed Description

A foldable group inside a page or a card: a header with a chevron, a title, a count and a subtitle, and the rows under it. Lighter than a KitCard, for lists split by kind — sounds by bus, versions of a changelog.

Give it a stateKey and the fold survives a rebuild of the page for the rest of the editor session. Give it a builder (SetContent) and the rows are built only when the group is first opened — a long folded list costs nothing.

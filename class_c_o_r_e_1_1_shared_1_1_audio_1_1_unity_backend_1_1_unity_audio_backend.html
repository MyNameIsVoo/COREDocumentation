Detailed Description

IAudioBackend on Unity audio, set up by a UnityAudioBackendAsset: a pool of AudioSources under a persistent root, voice limits per cue and overall, clips loaded from the bundles once and cached. Parameters: volume and pitch per instance; a global parameter sets the exposed mixer parameter of that name.

Clip memory: a preload loads the clips of a cue and their audio data; an unload drops a clip from the cache and unloads it from its bundle — never while a voice plays it.

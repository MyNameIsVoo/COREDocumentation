Classes

class  PlayerPairing
 The InputUser of one player and the devices paired to it (since v1.3) — the only place that pairs devices and associates actions with a user. The user's actions are restricted to its devices through InputUser.AssociateActionsWithUser (never a binding mask or a control scheme: a mask would drop every binding without a group). A player has one gamepad at a time — a new one replaces the old; keyboard and mouse pair to the primary only, a synthetic mouse (the virtual cursor's) too. The devices snapshot is replaced as a whole, never mutated.
 
class  PlayerRuntime
 What a joined player 1..N plays through (since v1.3): runtime copies of the primary's assets (same action ids, the binding overrides copied explicitly — they are not serialized), paired to the player's gamepad, with a mode service of its own (Gamepad from the start), a device service, an activation gate over the player's gamepad, haptics that follow the primary's settings and never fall back to another gamepad, and the virtual cursor when the settings ask for one. The copies' maps mirror the primary's enabled maps unless the player enabled or disabled one explicitly. Destroyed immediately on leave — the next join starts from a fresh copy.
 
class  PlayerService
 Local multiplayer (since v1.3): up to InputPlayersSettings.MaxPlayers players, each with a gamepad, a navigation context and a UI focus of their own; keyboard and mouse always belong to the primary. While the primary plays alone nothing of this costs anything — no UnityEngine.InputSystem.Users.InputUser, no copy of the assets, the host routes devices as usual. The first Join or EnableJoining pairs the primary too; when the last player leaves and joining is off, the single-player path is back. Joins and leaves requested inside an input update (from an action callback) are queued to the next tick. Reached through InputHost.Players. More...
 
class  PlayerSlot
 A player by index (since v1.3): a stable object whose subscriptions survive leaving and joining. The primary (index 0) is the host itself — its events are the host's, its actions the host's asset, keyboard and mouse are always its own. A player 1..N is joined while IsJoined: then it has a runtime copy of the assets restricted to its gamepad, a navigation context, haptics and, when the settings say so, a virtual cursor. More...
 
class  PlayerVirtualCursor
 The stick-driven cursor of a player 1..N (since v1.3): a simulated pointer with an id of its own (PointerId: UGUI keeps −1..−3 for the mouse buttons and 0.. for touches) over the player's copy of the VirtualCursor actions — no mouse device, the hardware cursor and the EventSystem selection are never touched.

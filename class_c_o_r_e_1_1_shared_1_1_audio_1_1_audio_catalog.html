Detailed Description

What an AudioKey resolves to. Three sources, later wins: the generated clips (one cue per clip file, its bus from AudioProjectSettings.BusRules), the cues of audio.json (AudioCueConfig) and runtime registrations (Register(AudioCue, object, int), higher priority wins — mods use their load order).

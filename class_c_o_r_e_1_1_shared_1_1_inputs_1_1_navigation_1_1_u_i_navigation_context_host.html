Detailed Description

The navigation contexts of the players and the scopes that route scene components to them (since v1.3). With no UINavigationScope alive, Resolve(Component) answers the primary context without walking the hierarchy — the single-player path costs nothing. Attached contexts are ticked by the host.

The contexts of players 1..N (local multiplayer): attached with a navigation, providers, buttons and hints of their own over the player's input, detached when the player leaves.

The navigation services of the attached contexts: created for the primary by the host in bootstrap order, ticked by index, disposed in reverse. The registry of enabled layers replaces a scene search.

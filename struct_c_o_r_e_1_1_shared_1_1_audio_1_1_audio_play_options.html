Detailed Description

How one instance plays: default plays the cue as authored. Multiplies the cue's volume and pitch; Position or Follow makes a spatial cue 3D at that point.

_audio.Play(AudioKeys.Door_Open, new AudioPlayOptions { Position = transform.position, Volume = 0.8f });

Detailed Description

A set of elements the stick walks through in one mode — a list, a grid, a ring (since v1.3). Belongs to the nearest UIInputLayer above it; links to neighbour groups hand the focus over at the edges or by direction. An ActivationAction makes the group enterable by a button from the "no group" state of a layer that allows it, an ExitAction leaves it the same way.

The hints of the group: highlighted while the group is active, or shown as its activation prompt. They may lie outside the group's hierarchy — the group assigns itself as their owner.

The elements of the group: collecting, ordering, the default and the next one in a direction.

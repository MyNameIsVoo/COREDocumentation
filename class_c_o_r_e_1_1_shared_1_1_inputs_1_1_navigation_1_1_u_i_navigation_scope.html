Detailed Description

Routes the layers, buttons and icons under it to a player's UINavigationContext (since v1.3). Goes on the root of a player's panel — on the layer's object or above it, never inside a layer. Without any scope in the scene every component belongs to the primary player and nothing walks the hierarchy.

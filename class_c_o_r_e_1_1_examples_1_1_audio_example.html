Detailed Description

Everything IAudioService does: a one-shot of the generated catalog, an audio.json cue with a random pitch and a cooldown, a loop held by its handle (fade in / out, pitch parameter), 3D sounds to the left and right, the player's bus volumes from the settings, a paused bus, a game-side bus scale and the music player. The buttons themselves click through their AudioComponent.

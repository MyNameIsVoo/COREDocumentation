Detailed Description

Puts the clip of a LocAssetKey in the current language on the AudioSource of its object (voice-over, a localized jingle) and swaps it on every language change. A source with no output mixer group is routed into the group of its Bus (the backend's mixer), so the player's volume of that bus applies. One-shots without a source of their own go through IAudioService.PlayClip.

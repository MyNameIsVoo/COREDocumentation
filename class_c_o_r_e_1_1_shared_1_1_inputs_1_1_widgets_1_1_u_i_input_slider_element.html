Detailed Description

A focusable slider (since v1.3): while it holds the focus, two actions move it — a step on the press, a steady move while held; both at once stand still. A whole-number slider steps by at least one and keeps the remainder of a held move so it does not stall. The slider's own axis belongs to it: the navigation does not take the focus away along it, and the stick steps the value with the navigation's repeat.

Detailed Description

A selection cycled by two actions — the shoulders through filter tabs, a category bar (since v1.3). The items register themselves and are ordered by sibling index; the selected one may be submitted on every transition. Works in the Gamepad mode while one of the target layers is active (always without target layers).

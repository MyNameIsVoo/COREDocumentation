Detailed Description

The Local players window (InputHost.Players): open the lobby — with JoinPolicy AnyButtonOnUnpairedGamepad any button on a free gamepad joins — or join the next free gamepad from code, let the last player leave. The right panel lies under a UINavigationScope of player 2: only that player's gamepad moves its focus, framed in the player's color, while player 1 stays in the left panel.

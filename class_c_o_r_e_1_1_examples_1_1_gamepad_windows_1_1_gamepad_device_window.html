Detailed Description

The Device and haptics window: the mode, the device and its family as they change; a short pulse and a steady rumble (InputHost.Haptics), vibration on and off and its strength (kept in Settings.Controls); the stick-driven virtual cursor — its buttons carry VirtualCursorMagnets that pull it in; a mouse warp that keeps the Gamepad mode (InputHost.SuppressMouseDeviceUsed); the Steam Deck readability — the sample under the haptics carries the four adapters (text, image, rect, layout group) and grows while it is on. Rebinding Jump is GamepadJumpRebind next to it.

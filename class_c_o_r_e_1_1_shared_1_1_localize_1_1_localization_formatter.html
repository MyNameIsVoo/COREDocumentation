Detailed Description

Fills the placeholders of a localized string:

  • {0}, {1:N0} — positional arguments, with an optional .NET format;
  • {name} — a named argument;
  • {count:plural:one=# item|other=# items} — the form of the CLDR category of the number (zero one two few many other, or an exact =0); # is the number, a form may hold placeholders;
  • {{ and }} — literal braces.

A placeholder without an argument stays in the text as it is.

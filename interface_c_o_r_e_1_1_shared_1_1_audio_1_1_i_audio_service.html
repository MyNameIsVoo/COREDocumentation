Detailed Description

The sound of the game, whatever engine plays it (AudioProjectSettings.Backend). Inject it:

[Zenject.Inject] private IAudioService _audio;
var handle = _audio.Play(AudioKeys.UI_MouseSingleClick_0);
_audio.Stop(handle, 0.5f);

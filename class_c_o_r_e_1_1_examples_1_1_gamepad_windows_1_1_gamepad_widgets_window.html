Detailed Description

The Widgets window: sliders (UIInputSliderElement — left and right move the value, the focus stays), a dropdown whose list is a nested layer (UIInputDropdownNavigation), an option switched by LB / RB (UIInputCycleController), a card that shows a tooltip on A (UIInputEmptyElement), a save row with two actions of its own while focused (UIInputDualButtonElement: X loads, Y deletes) and a panel entered with A and left with B (UIInputNestedLayerElement).

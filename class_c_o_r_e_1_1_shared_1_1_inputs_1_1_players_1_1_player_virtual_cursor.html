Detailed Description

The stick-driven cursor of a player 1..N (since v1.3): a simulated pointer with an id of its own (PointerId: UGUI keeps −1..−3 for the mouse buttons and 0.. for touches) over the player's copy of the VirtualCursor actions — no mouse device, the hardware cursor and the EventSystem selection are never touched. The image is tinted with the player's color. Same activation swallow frame and magnets as the primary's.

Detailed Description

Presses the UIInputButtons of one player (since v1.3): one subscription per action, hold timers, releases armed for ButtonUp buttons. Every press looks like a press: the Selectable stays pressed while the gamepad button is held, whatever the trigger. Silent outside the Gamepad mode, behind the activation gate, and for a press on the physical control that a navigation Back just consumed.

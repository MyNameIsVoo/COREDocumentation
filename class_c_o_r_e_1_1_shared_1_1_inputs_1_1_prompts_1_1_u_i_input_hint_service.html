Detailed Description

Highlights the hints that go with the focus of one player (since v1.3): the active layer's, the active element's, the active group's (except its activation prompts) — or, with no active group, the activation prompts of every group that can be entered. Gamepad mode only. Re-checks the activation prompts every LateUpdate: a group's availability changes without an event (a window became interactable after its opening animation).

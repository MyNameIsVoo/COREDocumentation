Detailed Description

The color library in the project. Stores all colors that are loaded from the settings (ColorLibraryData) and the palettes added on top of them (AddPalettes — every PlayerColorLibraryData under a Resources folder, a plugin's own colours). A colour is found by its generated enum tag or by its palette hash (TryGetColor(string, out ColorCustom)); the hash is the same in every project, the tag is not.

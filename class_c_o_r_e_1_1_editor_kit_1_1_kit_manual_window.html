Detailed Description

The frame of a Manual window: chapters by group on the left with a search over all of them, the chapter in the middle with links into the editor's windows, its headings on the right. A package derives it, adds a menu item and points KitHelp.Handler at OpenPage<T>, so every help button of every kit window opens it at the right chapter.

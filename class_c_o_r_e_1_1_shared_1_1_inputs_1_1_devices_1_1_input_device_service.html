Detailed Description

The device the player is playing with and the family of the active gamepad (since v1.3). Fed by InputDeviceUseDetector; when the active device is removed, falls back to the current gamepad, any gamepad, the keyboard, the native mouse — the mode follows through ActiveDeviceChanged.

Detailed Description

Entering and leaving a group by a button (since v1.3): for layers that allow an empty focus — "nothing selected" until a group's UIInputGroup.ActivationAction picks a group (a move does not: such a layer has no default group, so the stick and the D-pad stay free for the game); the UIInputGroup.ExitAction goes back to nothing. The bindings follow the active layer.

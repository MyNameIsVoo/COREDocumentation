Detailed Description

A settings asset of a package: a ScriptableSingleton<T> saved where its [FilePath] says — ProjectSettings/ for the team's, UserSettings/ for one person's — with a key/value store any package may write to under keys prefixed with its module id. A subclass adds typed fields and sets them through Apply, so every change is saved and announced.

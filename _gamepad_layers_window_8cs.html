class  CORE.Examples.GamepadWindows.GamepadLayersWindow
 The Nested layers window. The inventory is a UIInputNestedLayerElement two levels deep: A enters its slot grid, A on the pouch enters the pouch, B steps back one level at a time. The chest's items carry a GamepadChestLock: while locked the chest cannot be entered and its UIInputNestedLayerElementHint hides the A prompt. The dialog is a layer the game pushes from code — its UIInputNestedLayerBackRequester removes it on B. The notice is a VVM visual whose layer B hides through UIInputLayerBackHidesVisual. More...

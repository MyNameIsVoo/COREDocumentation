Detailed Description

The frame of a Debugger window: the pages of every included module in a sidebar, the picked one refreshed at most four times a second and only when its KitDebugPanel.Version moved. A package derives it and adds a menu item; the pages are KitDebugPanel subclasses found by themselves.

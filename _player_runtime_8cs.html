class  CORE.Shared.Inputs.Players.PlayerRuntime
 What a joined player 1..N plays through (since v1.3): runtime copies of the primary's assets (same action ids, the binding overrides copied explicitly — they are not serialized), paired to the player's gamepad, with a mode service of its own (Gamepad from the start), a device service, an activation gate over the player's gamepad, haptics that follow the primary's settings and never fall back to another gamepad, and the virtual cursor when the settings ask for one. The copies' maps mirror the primary's enabled maps unless the player enabled or disabled one explicitly. Destroyed immediately on leave — the next join starts from a fresh copy.

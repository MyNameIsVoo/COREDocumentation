Detailed Description

A player's UI navigation (since v1.3): a stack of UIInputLayers, the active group and element of the top one, and the moves. Works only in the Gamepad mode — the layers are cleared in Keyboard&Mouse and restored from the enabled auto-activating layers on the way back. The primary player's one is InputHost.Navigation, every player's is UINavigationContext.Navigation.

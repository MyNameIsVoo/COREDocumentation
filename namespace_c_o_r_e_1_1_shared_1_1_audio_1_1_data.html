enum CORE.Shared.Audio.Data.AudioRolloff

How the volume of a spatial cue falls with the distance between AudioCue.MinDistance and AudioCue.MaxDistance. Honoured by CORE's Unity backend; an FMOD event carries its own attenuation.

Enumerator
Logarithmic 

Unity's logarithmic curve (MinDistance / distance): never silent, past MaxDistance the sound stays at MinDistance / MaxDistance.

Linear 

A straight line from full volume at MinDistance to silence at MaxDistance.

LogarithmicCutoff 

The logarithmic curve, cut to silence at MaxDistance: a looped world sound stops being heard and costs nothing beyond its range.

Custom 

AudioCue.RolloffCurve: time 0–1 is the distance over MaxDistance, value the volume. For cues registered from code (AudioCatalog.Register(AudioCue, object, int)).

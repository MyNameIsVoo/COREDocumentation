Detailed Description

A shortcut button (since v1.3): a Selectable pressed by a gamepad action outside the focus of the navigation — "Back", "Apply", a tab switch. Fires on press, on release or after a hold (PressDuration with a fill), submits the Selectable, and shows itself only while its binding is live: in the Gamepad mode, on the attached layers, while one of the attached elements is focused. Registered with the binding service of its player's context.

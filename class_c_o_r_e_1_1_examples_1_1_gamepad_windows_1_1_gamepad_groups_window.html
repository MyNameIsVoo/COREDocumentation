Detailed Description

The Groups and restrictions window. Its layer may have no active group (AllowEmptyGroup, no default group): nothing is selected until a group's ActivationAction enters it — X the items, Y the actions, LB the quick bar — and the same button leaves (ExitAction); the UIInputGroupHints above the groups are those prompts. The items use GridScoring.Nearest and hand the focus to the actions at their right edge (OnEdgeOnly); from the actions Left always goes back (AlwaysByDirection); the quick bar keeps the focus — the arrows never leave it, LB does (ManualOnly). The window is also the game's IUIInputRestrictions: switched on, it vetoes every second item and the RT shortcut that sorts them.

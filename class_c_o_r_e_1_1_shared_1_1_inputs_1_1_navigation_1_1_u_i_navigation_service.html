Detailed Description

The navigation of one player (since v1.3): the layer stack, the active group and element, the moves. The top layer is decided by canvas sorting order and hierarchy, not by push order; rebuilds are deferred and merged and flushed in LateUpdate before the focus is validated. See IUINavigationService.

The focus of the navigation: the active layer, group and element, their presentation, the moves, submit and cancel, validation.

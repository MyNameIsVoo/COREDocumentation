Detailed Description

The hint of the layer, highlighted while the layer is active.

The groups of the layer and the move between them: the core of the directional navigation.

A screen or a panel the gamepad navigates — a stack entry of its player's navigation (since v1.3). Holds its groups and remembers its own active group and element. With AutoActivate it enters the stack when enabled in the Gamepad mode and leaves when disabled; the game pushes the others itself. The layer never closes a window: Cancel raises BackRequested, the window decides.

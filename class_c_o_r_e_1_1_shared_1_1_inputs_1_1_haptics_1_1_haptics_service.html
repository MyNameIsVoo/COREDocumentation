Detailed Description

Gamepad vibration of the primary player (since v1.3): a timed pulse (Play) or a continuous rumble (SetMotorSpeeds) on the active gamepad, scaled by MasterIntensity. A vibration moves with the active device to another gamepad and stops on a keyboard or on leaving the Gamepad mode (InputHapticsSettings.StopOnInputModeChange). With InputHapticsSettings.PersistSettings the player's choice lives in Settings.Controls (HapticsEnabled, HapticsIntensity) and follows every Settings.Apply. Reached through InputHost.Haptics; a player's service in local multiplayer follows the primary's settings and never falls back to a gamepad that is not its own.

The player's choice in Settings.Controls, with InputHapticsSettings.PersistSettings.

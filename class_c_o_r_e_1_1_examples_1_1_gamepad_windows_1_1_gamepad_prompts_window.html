Detailed Description

The Prompts and buttons window: shortcut buttons pressed from anywhere in the window (UIInputButton attached to its layer) — X on the press, Y after a one-second hold with a filling ring, RT on the release; the prompt bar (UIInputIcon with labels); a gallery of UIInputStandaloneIcons bound from code that redraw when another family of gamepad is picked up.

Detailed Description

The icon (and label) of an action inside a hint or next to a UIInputButton (since v1.3): shown for the active gamepad family while the player's prompts are live, the button may be shown and the hint allows icons; cleared otherwise. Follows the mode, the family, the lock, the hints' visibility and the language.

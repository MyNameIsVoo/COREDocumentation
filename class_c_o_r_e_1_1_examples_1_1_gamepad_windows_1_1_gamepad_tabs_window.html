Detailed Description

The Tabs and lists window: three tabs switched by LB / RB (UIInputTabController, the shoulders are UIInputButtons with their icons), the third one a long list in a scroll view — the focus keeps itself in view (UIInputScrollIntoView) and the right stick scrolls (UIInputScrollController). The active tab is framed, with the gamepad and with the mouse alike.

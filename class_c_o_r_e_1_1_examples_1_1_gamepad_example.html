Detailed Description

The Gamepad page: a hub — a UIInputLayer with a list of windows, each showing one side of the gamepad support (navigation, widgets, tabs and lists, prompts, the device and haptics, local players). A window is a layer of its own on top of the hub; B closes it and the focus comes back to the button that opened it (the hub layer's RememberLastFocus). The bottom line follows the mode, the device and its family, and every Back the navigation hands out (InputHost.BackRequested — after the layer's own event, for a game-wide rule).

Detailed Description

The navigation on the left of a window: foldable groups of items with a glyph and an optional count, one selected. Arrow keys move the selection when it has focus. Meant for tens of items — a long list of entries is a ListView.

Items added after AddGroup belong to that group. Give the sidebar a key and the folded groups are remembered across rebuilds and sessions. A group whose items are all hidden (Item.SetVisible) hides its header too.

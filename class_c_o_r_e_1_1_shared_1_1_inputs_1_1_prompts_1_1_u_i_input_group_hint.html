Detailed Description

The hint of a group (since v1.3): shown while the group is active, or — when the group has an UIInputGroup.ActivationAction — as the prompt to enter it while the layer has no active group. Requirements make it conditional: any listed element or group available, any listed transform with an active child. May lie outside the group's hierarchy: the group assigns itself as the owner.
